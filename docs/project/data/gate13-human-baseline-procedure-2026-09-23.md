# Gate 13 human-baseline procedure

**Version:** gate13-human-baseline-normal-tools-v1  
**Frozen:** 2026-09-23  
**Cohort:** complete 43-member U.S. transportation acceptance universe  
**Mode:** `NORMAL_TOOLS`

## Purpose

Measure the preparation time a competent human researcher needs to produce the same decision-useful prospect-research brief that Astra is asked to produce. The comparator is the human's normal workflow, not an artificially restricted workflow.

## Per-target procedure

1. Start the stopwatch only after the target/company assignment is visible and the researcher is ready to begin.
2. Use normal research tools: browser/search, the company's public website, public search results, ordinary note-taking, and the researcher's normal drafting tools. Do not use Astra or any output from the measured Astra attempt.
3. Research the same four frozen business fields used by Gate 13: `companyName`, `companySummary`, `transformationOpportunities`, and `buyingSignals`.
4. Preserve uncertainty. If a field cannot be supported from public evidence, record it as unknown rather than inventing an answer.
5. Stop the stopwatch when the brief is in the state the researcher would normally hand to a seller for use without additional prospect research.
6. Record the elapsed minutes immediately through the durable Gate 13 `MEASURED_HUMAN` baseline path before Astra is allowed to reserve/start that target.
7. Record the actual tooling description used for that target. Do not backfill or estimate baseline time after seeing the Astra result.

## Included time

Include search/navigation, source reading, evidence/note capture, synthesis, uncertainty handling, and final brief preparation.

## Excluded time

Exclude unrelated interruptions, meetings, deliberate idle time, and any later Astra evidence review/correction work. Astra-side human review is measured separately in the frozen outcome categories.

## Integrity rules

- Human baseline must precede the target's measured Astra reservation/run.
- The researcher must not see the measured Astra result before completing the baseline.
- Difficult or inaccessible companies remain in the cohort.
- Do not replace a slow human baseline or a failed Astra target after results are visible.
- Use the same procedure for all 43 targets.
